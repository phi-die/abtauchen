Thirty-One, the classic kitchen-table card game, as a dive for iPhone and iPad. Every round you survive takes you deeper: points are meters. House rules, card upgrades and bosses with rules of their own wait from the pool down to the trench.

Help

What's free? The pool and the lake, with the lifeguard and the pike. The full version (one-time, no subscription) opens the sea, the deep sea and the endless trench.

Full version gone on a new phone? Settings > Full version > "Restore purchases".

Got a code? Settings > Full version > "Redeem code".

Lost my run? The game saves after every round. On the home screen, tap "Keep diving".

Leaderboard missing? It runs on Game Center. Sign in to Game Center in your iPhone's settings.
